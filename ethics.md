# Ethics Statement

## Ethical Use

CIPHER-PU is an open-source research accelerator for Fully Homomorphic Encryption
(FHE) based on the CKKS scheme. This tool is designed to advance research in
privacy-preserving computation and post-quantum cryptography.

We intend for CIPHER-PU to be used for legitimate scientific research,
education, and industrial applications that respect individual privacy and
fundamental rights.

## Intended Uses

- Privacy-preserving machine learning research
- Post-quantum cryptography education and study
- Homomorphic encryption performance benchmarking
- Verification of HE security standards (HE Standard v1.1)

## Responsible Use

Users of this software are responsible for ensuring their applications comply
with applicable laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

- **Personal data protection** (GDPR, PIPL, CCPA, etc.)
- **Export controls** on cryptographic materials
- **Local laws** regarding encrypted computation

## Non-Intended Uses

CIPHER-PU is **not** intended to be used for:

- **Malicious surveillance** of individuals
- **Bypassing encryption** without lawful authority
- **Surveillance of minors** or vulnerable populations
- **Circumventing legal requirements** for encryption
- **Weaponization** of homomorphic encryption against protected groups

## AI Ethics

While CIPHER-PU accelerates encrypted inference of machine learning models, it
does **not** enable or endorse:

- **Discriminatory use** of encrypted ML
- **Bias amplification** in encrypted models
- **Deceptive applications** of privacy-preserving AI

## Research Ethics

All experiments reported in `reports/experiments/` were conducted with:

- **Synthetic or open-source datasets** (MNIST, CIFAR-10, synthetic vectors)
- **No personal data** was used in any benchmark
- **No human subjects** were involved

## Limitations and Bias

CIPHER-PU is a hardware accelerator, not an ethical framework. Users must:

1. Understand the **limitations of FHE** (e.g., security assumptions, performance constraints)
2. Recognize **biases in encrypted ML models** carried over from unencrypted training data
3. Consider **societal impact** of deploying encrypted computation in sensitive domains

## Data Privacy

- No personal data collected from users
- No telemetry or usage tracking
- Communication only through standard GitHub channels
